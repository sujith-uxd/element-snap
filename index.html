The problem

A full-screen screenshot is the wrong size every time

You take the whole screen, open an editor, crop to the one card you wanted and paste. Chrome has a built-in way to capture a single node, but it's buried inside DevTools, four clicks deep. Element Snap puts that one action on the page itself.

What it does

Pick the element, get the picture

Capture one element

Hover any part of a page and it's outlined with crop marks. Click it, or press Space, and it's copied.

Full height

An element taller than the screen is scrolled through view and stitched into one image. The page is put back where it was.

Several at once

Shift-click elements to mark them, numbered 1, 2, 3. Each one becomes its own shot.

Any area

Press and drag to draw a box when what you want isn't a single element.

Space keeps menus open

Space doesn't touch the page, so open menus, tooltips and hover states stay as they look.

Works inside Claude artifacts

Elements inside published artifacts can be picked and captured like any other page.

Stays out of the way

The panel sits above a page's pop-ups, dialogs and overlays, and clicking it never closes them. Up to 50 shots are kept across pages: copy one, copy all, download all as PNG files, or drag a thumbnail into another app.

How it works

Three steps, no editor

01

Start picking

Click the toolbar icon, press AltShiftS, or press Capture in the panel. A hint bar at the top of the page lists the controls.

02

Hover and click

The element under the mouse gets crop marks and a label with its size. Click it, or press Space, and it's copied and added to the panel.

03

Paste or drag

Paste it where you need it with CtrlV or ⌘V, or drag the thumbnail out of the panel into another app.

Pasting

Works with your apps

Chrome can put one picture on the clipboard at a time. Apps that read rich content get every shot as its own image. Apps that take only pictures get one combined image, so for those use Download all or drag the thumbnails one by one.

Install

About two minutes, no account, no coding

Element Snap loads as an unpacked folder, so Chrome runs it from wherever you unzip it.

  1. Unzip the downloadYou get a folder called element-snap. Keep it where it is, because Chrome loads the extension from it.
  2. Turn on Developer modeOpen chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode, top right.
  3. Load the folderClick Load unpacked and choose the element-snap folder.
  4. Pin itOpen the puzzle-piece menu in the toolbar and pin Element Snap, so its icon is always there.

Updating

Replace the folder with the new one, then click the reload icon on Element Snap's card in chrome://extensions. Refresh any open Claude artifacts afterwards.

Limits

What it can't do yet

Where doesn't it run?

Chrome blocks every extension on its own pages: chrome:// pages, the Chrome Web Store and the new-tab page.

Why did nothing happen on a new page?

Click the icon once on that page before capturing. Chrome only lets an extension screenshot pages where its icon was clicked.

Does full height work sideways?

No. It works up and down only, and elements wider than the screen are cut at the edge.

Why does a header repeat in a tall shot?

Sticky headers inside the scrolling area appear in every part of a stitched shot. Content that loads as you scroll may come out blank.

Can it pick inside embedded content?

Embedded content from other sites can't be picked into, except Claude artifacts. Marked elements must all be on the page, or all inside one artifact.

Is it on the Chrome Web Store?

No. It installs by loading the unpacked folder, which needs no account and no review. It also hasn't been checked on a Retina screen yet.
